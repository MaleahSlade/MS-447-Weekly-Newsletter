WHAT'S HAPPENING AT MS 447:

REMINDER: HALF-DAY AND STUDENT-LED CONFERENCES ON THURSDAY, NOV 12 🗓️

Students will dismiss tomorrow at 11:57 am. Optional lunch will be available in the cafeteria after dismissal. Virtual Student-led Conferences (SLC) are being scheduled in the afternoon or evening. Contact your student’s Pack or Advisory Leader if you have questions about the schedule and Zoom links.
 

REMINDER: SPELLING BEE ON FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 13 🐝

MS 447's annual Spelling Bee is on Friday, November 13, starting immediately after school in the auditorium!

If your student is planning to participate, even if they attended the information session and picked up materials, please have them RSVP using this form to confirm their participation.

If your student hasn't signed up yet but would like to join the bee, there's still time! Please have them complete the registration form and use the study list linked at the top. They can also contact Ms. Hertz-Velazquez (shertzvelazques@ms447.org).

Students who are NOT participating in the bee must be accompanied by a parent or guardian if they wish to observe. We are also looking for a few parent/guardian volunteers to help with easy event logistics (registering participants, handing out numbers, setting up, assisting students to their correct seats, etc.). If you would like to volunteer, please email Ms. Hertz-Velazquez (shertzvelazquez@ms447.org).

SURVEY FOR SOCCER ⚽

The PTA and MS 447 school administration are exploring opportunities to offer a soccer program for students. Before making any decisions, we'd love to hear from families about their interest, current access to soccer programs, and potential barriers to participation.


Your feedback will help us assess demand and explore how to make the program as inclusive and accessible as possible. Please complete the survey here.
