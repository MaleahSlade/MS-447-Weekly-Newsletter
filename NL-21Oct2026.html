WHAT'S HAPPENING AT MS 447:

OCTOBER FAMILY POTLUCK & PTA MEETING ON THURSDAY 🥘

Connect with other families and school staff at our upcoming potluck and PTA meeting! We’ll discuss the grading system, homework policy, Google Classroom and JumpRope. Bring a dish or drinks to share if you can. Sign up in Konstella to let us know what you plan to bring. And kids are welcome too!

  • When: Thursday, October 22, from 6:00 – 7:45 pm
  • Where: 3rd Floor Cafeteria
  • What: Meeting Agenda

Please enter through 345 Dean Street and bring your ID to sign in with security. We look forward to sharing a meal and connecting with you!

OPEN HOUSE FOR 5TH GRADE FAMILIES 👥

Help us help 5th grade families get to know our school by volunteering at our first Open House. You'll be paired with a teacher and help them with their tour group. Meet future MS 447 parents and have fun showing them around our school.

  • Oct. 24 Open House, 9:15 am -1:00 pm

  • Nov. 17 Open House, 3:45 pm - 5:30 pm

Questions about the events? Contact Parent Coordinator Amy Sirot (asirot@ms447.org).
