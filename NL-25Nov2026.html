WHAT'S HAPPENING AT MS 447:

A MESSAGE OF APPRECIATION FOR THE MS 447 COMMUNITY 💛🧡

Dear MS 447 Famillies,

Our Fall Staff Appreciation Breakfast was a wonderful success, and we couldn’t have done it without the generosity and support of our MS 447 families!

Thank you to everyone who contributed funds, donated delicious treats, and volunteered their time to help make the morning special. Whether you made a donation, brought something to share, or lent a helping hand with setup, serving, or cleanup, your efforts helped create a meaningful opportunity for our staff to feel recognized and appreciated.

Most importantly, we want to extend our heartfelt gratitude to the incredible MS 447 staff. Day in and day out, our teachers, administrators, and school staff work tirelessly to provide our students with a high-quality education, challenge them to reach their potential, and create an environment where they can learn, grow, and thrive. Their commitment extends far beyond the classroom, and we are grateful for the time, care, and dedication they bring to our school community.

It takes a village to support a school, and this breakfast was a beautiful reminder of what we can accomplish when families come together. Thank you for helping us show our staff just how much they mean to our MS 447 community.

We wish you a wonderful Thanksgiving break.

Warmly,

The MS 447 PTA

INTERPRETATION NOW AVAILABLE AT PTA MEETINGS: PICK YOUR LANGUAGE! 🗣️ 💬

From now on, live digital interpretation will be available during online PTA meetings. This means that you will be able to read in your preferred language what everyone is saying.
