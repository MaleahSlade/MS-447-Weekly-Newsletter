REMINDER: PARAMETERS FOR HALLOWEEN AT MS 447 🎃

On Friday, October 30th, students are permitted to come to school in costume. During the 8th period, students who are dressed up will have an opportunity to march in a parade through the school, showing off their awesome creativity to the rest of the students! To help make the day safe and enjoyable for everyone, please make sure that the costume your child wears follows the guidelines below:

  • No weapons are permitted.
  • No masks, except during the parade.
  • Eyes must be visible and uncovered at all times.
  • Students must enter school already dressed up; they cannot spend class time getting into costume.
  • Large costume props should be set aside in the classroom until the parade.
  • Glasses, wigs, moustaches, face paint, and costume masks that can be removed during the day are okay.
  • Costumes must not be offensive or culturally insensitive.

FUNDRAISING UPDATE ⚠️

Reaching our fundraising goal is essential to sustaining the programs that make MS 447 special. Every dollar contributed directly supports our students, with two critical PTA initiatives taking center stage right now:

  • The 447 Community Fund: Helping ensure every student can participate in school activities, Expo trips, and grade-wide experiences, regardless of a family’s ability to pay.

  • Athletics & Morning Gym: Providing gym access and reliable athletic equipment so our kids can start their school day active, energized, and ready to learn!

How You Can Help:

  • Make A Direct Contribution: Whether you give $10, $25, $100, $447 or any amount that fits your family's budget, 100% of your tax-deductible gift goes straight to work for our kids. And please consider making it an automatic monthly contribution! Donate here!

  • Corporate Matching: Many employers match charitable donations, doubling or even tripling your impact! Check whether your company offers a matching gift program. Need help? Email 447fundraising@gmail.com, and we’ll be happy to assist!
