WHAT'S HAPPENING AT MS 447:

OCTOBER PTA MEETING 🗣️

The next PTA Meeting will be held in person on Thursday, October 22, from 6:30 pm - 7:45 pm. Come to learn more about school updates, upcoming events, PTA initiatives, and opportunities to get involved in our school community. To attend, enter at 345 Dean Street and bring your ID to sign in with security. The meeting will take place in the 3rd Floor Cafeteria.
 

JUNIOR VARSITY BOYS BASKETBALL TRYOUTS 🏀

Junior varsity boys' basketball tryouts are TODAY, Wednesday, October 14, and Friday, October 16, at 3:00 pm in the gym. This is for 6TH AND 7TH GRADERS ONLY. Questions? Contact Coach Dallas (dalexis@ms447.org).
 

UPCOMING PICTURE DAYS 📸

Picture Days will be held on Tuesday, October 20 ( classes 602, 603, 604, 605, 606, 701, 704, 706, 802, 804) and Wednesday, October 21 (all other classes). Students do not need to bring forms or payment.
 

BILINGUAL PARENT VOLUNTEERS REQUESTED 👥

MS 447 is looking for Spanish- and Chinese-speaking parents and guardians to volunteer at our upcoming open houses for 5th-grade families. Volunteers will accompany a small group of families during the presentation and school tour, helping them understand the information shared along the way. No formal interpreting experience is necessary, just a willingness to help fellow families feel welcome and informed! Please sign up here for one or more open houses:

  • Oct. 24 Open House, 9:15 am -1:00 pm
  • Nov. 9 Open House, 6:00 pm - 7:30 pm (online)
  • Nov. 17 Open House, 3:45 pm - 5:30 pm

Questions about the event? Contact Parent Coordinator Amy Sirot (asirot@ms447.org).
 

SCRIPPS SPELLING BEE 🐝

MS 447 is participating in the Scripps Spelling Bee again this year. Interested students can go to room 333 after school to learn more. Email Ms. Hertz-Velazquez (shertzvelazques@ms447.org) with any questions.
