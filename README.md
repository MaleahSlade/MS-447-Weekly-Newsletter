MS 447 Weekly Newsletter

Project Overview

A weekly HTML/CSS newsletter designed to keep the MS 447 school community informed and engaged. Developed for an audience of 1,000+ parents and teachers, each newsletter combines responsive design, user experience (UX) principles, and thoughtful visual design to deliver school updates and important information in a clear, accessible, and visually engaging format.

Objectives

* Responsive Design: Develop HTML/CSS newsletter templates that adapt to different screen sizes and provide a consistent reading experience across devices.
* User-Centered Design: Apply UX principles to organize information intuitively, prioritize important updates, and create an accessible digital experience.
* Visual Communication: Balance aesthetically pleasing design with readability and functionality to make school communications engaging and easy to navigate.
* Community Engagement: Collaborate with school leadership and families to coordinate and communicate critical information, helping keep stakeholders informed and connected.

Skills & Technologies

* HTML5
* CSS3
* Responsive Web Design
* User Experience (UX) Principles
* Accessible Digital Communication
* Visual Design & Information Hierarchy
* Technical Communication

Audience

The MS 447 school community, including more than 1,000 parents and teachers.

Project Focus

This project brings together front-end development, visual design, and technical communication to create a practical digital product that meets the information needs of a diverse school community.
