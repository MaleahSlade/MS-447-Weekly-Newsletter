MATCHING FUNDS WILL DOUBLE YOUR IMPACT ON GIVING TUESDAY 🎁

As the holiday season approaches and you begin thinking about your end-of-year giving, we hope you’ll consider supporting MS 447. With Giving Tuesday just two weeks away on December 1, there’s an exciting opportunity to make your contribution go even further!

We are thrilled to announce a $5,000 Matching Challenge!

An anonymous donor has generously pledged to match the first $5,000 raised between now and Giving Tuesday (December 1), doubling the impact of every donation! That means a $50 gift becomes $100, and a $100 gift becomes $200. Reaching the full $5,000 match would bring our fundraising total to $16,000 since the start of the school year!

🚨🚨DONATE HERE TO DOUBLE YOUR IMPACT🚨🚨

Why Your Support Matters:

Your contributions help sustain the programs and experiences that make MS 447 special and are a big part of why so many families choose our school. These include our Signature Expo Trips, which take classroom learning beyond school walls and bring it to life through hands-on experiences throughout NYC and beyond.

Every contribution makes a difference, and this matching challenge is a wonderful opportunity to make your gift go twice as far. All donations are 100% tax-deductible with one-time and recurring monthly options available.

Thank you for your continued generosity and for supporting the MS 447 community!

With gratitude,

The MS 447 PTA

INTERPRETATION NOW AVAILABLE AT PTA MEETINGS: PICK YOUR LANGUAGE! 🗣️ 💬

From now on, live digital interpretation will be available during online PTA meetings. This means that you will be able to read in your preferred language what everyone is saying. Don’t feel comfortable speaking English? No problem. When you speak in your preferred language, everyone else will be able to read what you say in their preferred language. Zoom enables digital interpretation into Spanish, Chinese, and many other languages. We’re so excited that language will no longer be a barrier to participation!
