REMINDER: 6TH GRADE OVERNIGHT CAMP TRIP THIS WEEK! 🎒 ⚠️

If your child is attending the 6th grade overnight camp trip, please review these important reminders and instructions from school administration:

THURSDAY, 11/5: ARRIVAL TIME IS 7:15 AM!

Students should arrive at 345 Dean Street at 7:15 AM. They will drop off their luggage and sleeping bags in their designated areas before heading to the auditorium.

Important: Parents who are not chaperoning the trip may not enter the building that morning. Please drop your child off at the front of the school.

BREAKFAST & PHONES

  • Breakfast will NOT be provided on Thursday morning. Please make sure your child eats breakfast before arriving. Students are also welcome to bring breakfast to eat on the bus.
  • Phones are permitted on the bus but will be collected and secured in Yondr pouches for the duration of the camp stay.

PACK FOR COLD WEATHER!

Temperatures will be cold, so please make sure your child is prepared with warm clothing and appropriate outdoor footwear.

The attached packing list includes everything students should bring, including a sleeping bag. We recommend comfortable, closed-toe shoes that can handle rain, mud, and dirt.

Need help getting gear for your child? Please contact Parent Coordinator Amy Sirot at asirot@ms447.org.

FOOD & OTHER ITEMS

  • NO NUTS OR SNACKS CONTAINING NUTS. Please be mindful that students with nut allergies will be attending the trip.
  • Please leave video game consoles, valuables, and money at home. Students will not need money while at camp.

FRIDAY, 11/6: RETURN TRIP

The buses are expected to depart camp between 12:45 and 1:00 PM on Friday. Students will have access to their phones once they are back on the bus and will contact families with an estimated arrival time. The school will also send an email with an ETA.

NEED TO REACH YOUR CHILD DURING THE TRIP?

If you need to contact your child while they are at camp:

  • During regular school hours: Call MS 447 at 718-330-9328. School staff will connect you with the 447 team at camp.
  • Outside of school hours: Call Camp Fairview Lake at 973-383-9282.

QUESTIONS OR CONCERNS?

Please reach out to Hera Yoon (hyoon@ms447.org) or Vassili Frantzis (vfrantzis@ms447.org).

We hope our 6th graders have a wonderful time at camp!
